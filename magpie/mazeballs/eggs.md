# Eggs, and reproduction as a process in time and space

Design notes on making reproduction physical instead of a point-event — and on
eggs, which turn out to be more foundational than they look. Companion to
`energy-speculative-friction.md` (the conservation law these all obey) and
`METHODS.md` (brain-is-body, one developmental encoding). No new first-class
primitive is introduced: everything here is cells + bonds + fields + energy +
a boundary.

## 1. Reproduction should exist in time and space (it deletes a shortcut)

Today reproduction is a point-event: fuel crosses a threshold, a child pops out
adjacent, instantly. That is a `predatorSpeed`-class shortcut — a high-level act
(making an offspring) implemented as a special primitive instead of falling out of
low-level physics. Make it a **process extended in time and space**: an offspring
is a *developing bonded cluster* that

- takes many steps to build (time),
- occupies a place (space),
- pays energy continuously over that time (the conserved budget), and
- **can fail partway** (no guaranteed child).

This is not a reproduction primitive. It is development + bonds + energy + a
boundary, pointed at building a *new* body instead of growing the current one.
Reproduction becomes "cells doing cell things, localised and extended." That is
the honest version, and it is the precondition for everything below.

## 2. Polyembryony — amortise the *setup*, not the material

If instantiating a body plan is serious, useful work, why get only one copy from
a developmental run? Real biology already does this: armadillos make identical
quads; the wasp *Copidosoma* makes **thousands** of clones from one egg (obligate
polyembryony). But the friction law forces a precise distinction about what is
amortised:

- **Material cost is NOT amortised** — every copy's cells still cost full energy to
  build. No free copies. (Minting would be fiction.)
- **The regulatory *setup* IS** — the morphogen field, the symmetry-breaking, the
  GRN cascade were established once. A developing mass carrying a Turing-like
  pattern of *N* peaks can fission into *N* body-axes off the **same** established
  chemistry. You paid to build the gradient once; it patterns several bodies.

That passes the friction test cleanly (the amortised thing was paid for once and
does more work — not minted). And it makes clutch size **emergent, not a knob**:
rich + warm conditions grow a bigger, faster-developing embryonic mass (energy
funds the build; warmth multiplies the rate — see the dev-rate rule in the energy
doc) → more morphogen peaks it can support → more copies. **"Sugar / heat / water
causes triplets, quads" becomes a consequence of energy × patterning**, never a
life-history parameter you set.

And it is **self-limiting for free**, via Lack's principle (clutch-size
optimisation): fixed yolk ÷ *N* means more copies → each weaker → an optimum
evolution finds. Rich conditions do not give unbounded quads; they *raise the
budget* so you can afford more copies at the same per-copy quality. Conserved
parental energy split among offspring gives you the quantity/quality trade-off
automatically.

## 3. Eggs — a container, a yolk, and (the deep one) a chemical chamber

An egg is three lawful things at once:

1. **A container** — a boundary (an inert bond-shell; dead cells / matter, no DNA,
   like the boats and firewood in WORLD.md) that keeps the developing cells
   together and separates inside-chemistry from the outside world.
2. **A yolk** — packaged parental energy, so development runs *without foraging*.
   Conserved: the parent spent fuel to provision it. A straw from parent to child;
   passes the friction law. It also lets a body develop to size **before** it must
   forage, front-loading energy past the surface/volume power wall during the
   vulnerable build (see the eukaryote-wall note in the energy doc).
3. **A contained chemical chamber** — the deep one. In the open turbulent soup,
   morphogen gradients get advected and diffused away by the very field dynamics
   that make the world interesting, so **reliable complex body plans are nearly
   impossible to develop in the open.** A boundary that contains the chemistry
   gives *stable* gradients → *repeatable* patterning. An egg is a controlled
   developmental chamber. "Cells divide within it, bobble around attracting /
   repelling on chemical dynamics" is exactly self-organisation under
   attraction/repulsion inside a *contained* morphogen field — development in a
   protected lab.

### Eggs may be a transition, not a feature

Because reliable large, patterned bodies may *require* the stable chemical
environment a container provides, the egg is a candidate **ceiling-breaker on the
staircase** (see `ASCENT.md`) — the enabling step for the whole reliable-complex-
development rung, which is also where brain-in-body patterning needs stable
gradients to lay out modular, symmetric connectivity. Reliable complex development
in the open may simply be unavailable; containment may be the lawful key that opens
it. That would make "the first egg" a major transition of the same kind as the
first body.

### An egg is also a Markov blanket

A body with a boundary is, statistically, a Markov blanket separating internal
states from the world — the thing that makes an "agent" describable as *inferring*
at all (see the free-energy / predictive-processing thread). So the egg-as-chamber
and the free-energy story are the same story: the developmental boundary and the
inferential boundary are one object. Containment is where agency, in the
predictive-processing sense, physically begins.

## 4. Discipline — so eggs don't become a safe box

- Keep egg / clutch-size / yolk-amount **emergent, not knobs** — from a boundary
  mechanism (bond-shell), a conserved parental transfer, contained fields, and
  development running inside. Never a "lay N eggs" parameter.
- **No free lunch.** Yolk is finite conserved parental energy; eggs are vulnerable
  matter (can be eaten, damaged, chilled — chilled = slow development via the Q10
  warmth rule); development can fail. An egg that could not fail would mint safety.
- Clutch size = yolk ÷ *N* with the Lack trade-off, never a set number.
- The shell is **inert** (matter, no DNA) — it decays, it can be firewood, it is
  not a living cheat.

## 5. Summary

Reproduction extended in time and space; polyembryony as amortised-*setup*,
emergent-and-Lack-bounded; and eggs as a containment **transition** — all three fit
the existing substrate (bonds, fields, energy, development) with **no new
first-class primitive**, and all three pass the conservation-and-closure law. The
egg is the sharpest of the three: it is simultaneously a physical container, a
conserved energy package, a stable developmental chamber, and a Markov blanket —
which is why it may be the lawful key that makes reliable complex bodies (and the
brains grown inside them) possible at all.
