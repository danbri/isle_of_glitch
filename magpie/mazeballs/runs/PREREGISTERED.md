# Pre-registered hypothesis, written before the run (2026-08-07)

**H1.** Enzyme diversity (`enzSd`) is biotically dominated at deep time:

    biotic  = |full - nobiotic|   >   abiotic = |full - noabiotic|

and the gap clears the pooled standard error, at 450 ticks (112k steps).

**Why this trait and no other.** A digestive enzyme's value depends entirely on
what other organisms are made of — `dietWidth` matches enzyme against tag — so it
is meaningless in a world of one. It is also a SPREAD rather than a level, which
makes it insensitive to the wealth confound that invalidated the toughness
reading in batch 1.

**Why this is not the twelve-way comparison again.** Batch 1 tested six traits at
two horizons and `enzSd`-at-deep was the single pass, which is the null
expectation for twelve tests and was reported as chance. This is ONE hypothesis,
fixed in advance, on an INDEPENDENT sample: five fresh replicates at seeds
disjoint from batch 1.

**The bar, fixed in advance.** H1 is supported only if the deep-time gap clears
the pooled SE in this batch on its own. Batch 1 does not count toward it and is
not pooled in — it generated the hypothesis and cannot also test it.

**What would falsify it.** Any of: biotic <= abiotic; the gap inside one pooled
SE; or fewer than four replicates surviving to the deep horizon.

Batch 1 result being retested: full 0.1795, nobiotic 0.2699, noabiotic 0.2209,
biotic 0.0905 against abiotic 0.0415, se 0.0414.
