Designed creatures

Ten bodies designed by specification and search: a name, a sentence saying what it is for, and a score over the body that develops. You cannot hand-write a regulatory network — a weight has no local meaning and no gene is a limb — so the intent is stated in the phenotype's own terms and the search finds a genome that grows into it. Ordinary genomes; nothing here is a new type or a role the kernel knows about.

A hand of god: it hands out yolk nobody paid for, so it mints energy — the one thing the world may not do on its own. Every use is written to the observation log and counted, because a run that has been meddled with is not a clean observation of evolution and has to say so.

It earns its keep by separating a design from its luck. A genome that dies out because it landed in the mud is not the same as one that cannot make a living, and watching cannot tell those apart. A hundred copies of the same body against coast, upland, channel and open water at once can. At mutation 0 they are literal clones, which is the cleanest form of the question.

Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom. Positions are continuous f32 and the flow is an analytic curl field — there is no grid to zoom past. Every cell is a CTRNN node in one shared arena; sensors write their own brain input and muscles contract their bonds, all without leaving the GPU.
