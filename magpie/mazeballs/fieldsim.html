This is the primitive WORLD.md is built on: one scalar field that diffuses and decays, advected by a divergence-free current from curl noise. A released smell is carried downstream and stretched into a plume — exactly the structured, followable gradient a body would have to sense to find its source against the flow. The design question to judge by eye: does this make sensing worth it — is upstream findable — in a way a uniform scatter never was?
