# Energy as speculative friction

> **Still current.** The conservation law, in full. Still binding — see [`WORLD-MANUAL.md`](WORLD-MANUAL.md) for how it
> sits in the world as a whole, and for the measurements behind it.

The point of taking energy seriously is not realism. It is **friction** — a law
that *resists* arbitrary invention, so that not every construct we can imagine can
be paid for. Energy is the thing that can tell a design **no**. This note is the
law that makes it do that, and the rules for staying inside it.

## The trap: loose energy is reward shaping in a lab coat

If you can pay for any behaviour by inventing a source, and forbid any behaviour
by inventing a sink, then "energy" constrains nothing. You would be shaping
outcomes through cost knobs instead of behaviour knobs — the `predatorSpeed` sin
(WORLD.md, THE FIRST LAW) through the back door, dressed as thermodynamics. Almost
every way of "adding energy" to a sim is this: decoration you can always satisfy.

So the danger is real. **The books can always be balanced by fiat — unless a law
forbids it.**

## The law: never mint, always conserve. Invent topology, not energy.

You may invent the **plumbing** of flows freely — new fields, couplings,
capture-channels, windfalls. That is the creative act and it is where transitions
come from. You may **not** invent energy. Four rules make this bind:

1. **One common currency.** Movement, division, differentiation, wiring, sensing,
   muscle — all draw from the *same* per-cell pool. They cannot be tuned
   independently because they share a budget. Costs genuinely trade off.
2. **One fixed boundary inflow.** Energy enters only through defined sources (the
   sun — a *world* constant), and leaves only as heat / dissipation / death. Every
   joule a cell spends traces back to captured external gradient.
3. **Every conversion loses** (efficiency < 1; 2nd law). The system runs *down* a
   gradient. Complexity is paid by continuous throughput and **decays when the flux
   stops** — it is never free-standing.
4. **Nothing mints.** No mechanism may create currency to balance its own books.
   If a construct only works because it quietly adds energy, it is fiction — delete
   it or trace its energy to the boundary.

Under these, balancing the books is not the designer's job; it is physics'. A
mechanism either fits under the fixed power budget or it does not.

## The physics / fiction line: global-uniform is legitimate, local-targeted is cheating

- **You may set the sun.** Global inflow is one knob that scales carrying capacity
  for *everyone*. It cannot privilege predators over prey; it moves the whole
  ceiling uniformly. Legitimate.
- **You may not grant energy to a capability.** "Bodies get +X so multicellularity
  pays" mints, and privileges a chosen outcome. Fiction.
- **A windfall is a new straw into the same drink, never a new drink.** The
  tough-food field is legitimate *only* because it is light-fed and conserved — a
  channel to the same sun, gated on a capability (a body). It is the plumbing, not
  a new source. Every proposed windfall must pass this test: *whose fixed gradient
  does it tap, and where does the loss go?*

## The test: "can't afford it" is the constraint working

A correct energy model is one where you **frequently fail to afford things you
wanted.** The brain tax bites; the surface/volume wall bites; a capability you'd
like simply cannot be built under the current sun. That refusal is the accounting
doing real work. **If nothing is ever unaffordable, you have minted somewhere.**

We already lived the failure mode in miniature. The multicellular tough-food
windfall *added* energy, so "bigger population" could not be read as ascent — the
free-energy term explains any improvement. It became evidence only once the
**same-world ancestral tournament / monoculture assay** stripped the free energy
out (both origins in one conserved world, so the extra flux cancels). That control
*is* the guard against energy-as-fiction, and it is why the instrument had to be
un-confounded. Any new energy mechanism inherits the same obligation: **the loop
must run the conserved-control, not just the enriched treatment.**

## Development rate: sugar gates the build, warmth multiplies the rate

Two physically distinct roles, and the pair is nearly free given existing fields:

- **Sugar (energy) is the budget — *can* you build it.** Gate every developmental
  event (divide, differentiate, grow a neuron, extend an axon) on fuel surplus.
- **Warmth is the rate multiplier — *how fast* the chemistry runs.** Q10 kinetics
  (~2–3× / 10°) on the whole rate layer, driven by the existing heat field
  (radiation = light + mutagen + **heat**). They **multiply**: a cold well-fed
  embryo builds slowly; a warm starving one cannot build at all. Emergent payoff: a
  life-history axis for free — warm-poor → small fast (r-selected); cool-rich → big
  slow (K-selected).

If only one goes in first, **eat sugar** — because that is what makes **Cherniak
fall out of Lane**. Energy-gated development + neurons with positions ⇒ a long axon
costs more to build and maintain than a short one ⇒ wiring minimisation,
modularity, and short-connection-dominant brains **emerge because long wires are
metabolically expensive**, not because we penalise length. Cherniak's wiring
optimisation is just Lane's energy accounting once neurons occupy space.

## How far without rebuilding: the accounting is cheap, the mechanism is not

Most of Nick Lane is *accounting*, and accounting is additive terms on the scalar
fuel we already have. Take the accounting all the way; defer the mechanism.

**Cheap / now (no rewrite):** every capability costs fuel; energy-gate development
(→ wiring cost + brain tax become literal); warmth as a Q10 multiplier.

**Medium (one new scalar, still no rewrite):**
- **Power vs stock.** Lane's real limit is energy *per gene* — throughput, not a
  hoard. Track a per-cell power meter; cap maintainable complexity by sustained
  power.
- **The eukaryote wall as geometry.** Power capture scales with membrane
  (perimeter ∝ *r* in 2D); maintenance scales with volume (area ∝ *r²*); so
  power-per-unit ∝ 1/*r* — bigger cells starve their own complexity. Lane's
  energy-per-gene ceiling as a pure size term.
- **Endosymbiosis, discovered not declared.** A cell type providing *internal*
  power makes power scale with volume, not surface → the 1/*r* wall lifts → a big
  genome/brain becomes affordable. The eukaryote windfall, as scalar
  power-decoupled-from-surface. No proton pump required — and note it still obeys
  the law: the internal power units capture the *same* boundary inflow, they do not
  mint.

**Expensive (rebuild), only for the *mechanism* not the consequence:** real
chemiosmosis — energy as a proton gradient across a membrane, charged by redox,
discharged to do work; electron transport; literal engulfment. Do this only when
you want eukaryogenesis to *emerge from bioenergetics* rather than be a modelled
(conserved) windfall.

## Checklist for any new energy mechanism (the loop must apply this)

1. **Whose fixed gradient does it tap, and where does the loss go?** (No answer ⇒
   it mints ⇒ reject.)
2. **Is the grant global-uniform (sets carrying capacity) or local-targeted
   (privileges an outcome)?** Local-targeted ⇒ reject.
3. **Does it ever leave something unaffordable?** If nothing is, energy leaked in
   somewhere ⇒ find it.
4. **Is there a conserved control** (same-world / monoculture) that removes the new
   flux, so any measured gain is not just the extra energy? If not, build it before
   believing the result.

Never mint, always conserve. Invent flow *topology*, never flow *from nothing*.
That single law is the difference between energy as physics and energy as
decoration — and it is the friction that stops the world from paying for whatever
we happen to imagine.
